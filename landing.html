FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What is Aadhaar eSign and is it legally valid?
Aadhaar eSign is an electronic signature service that uses your Aadhaar OTP for authentication. It is legally valid under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and is equivalent to a handwritten signature. Documents signed via Aadhaar eSign are court-admissible across India.
What is eStamping and how is it different from physical stamp paper?
eStamping is a secure online method for paying stamp duty to the government on legal documents. It eliminates the need to purchase physical stamp paper, saving you time and eliminating fraud risk. Each e-stamp has a unique identification number (UIN) that can be verified online.
Which regions in Gautam Buddh Nagar do you support?
We support all sectors and villages under Noida Authority, Greater Noida Industrial Development Authority (GNIDA), and Yamuna Expressway Authority (YEIDA). e-Stamps are generated instantly for Gautam Buddh Nagar district.
Can I edit my details after e-stamp paper is generated?
No. Due to security and legal reasons, editing details after e-stamp generation is not possible. Please double-check all information before proceeding. Once stamp paper is generated, stamp duty paid cannot be refunded.
How long does it take to draft a document?
Most documents take under 3 minutes to draft with our smart form system. You fill in the required details, and our system auto-generates a legally valid draft. You can preview, edit, and sign immediately — all without visiting any office.
Is my data safe on INSTADEED?
Absolutely. All data is encrypted with 256-bit AES encryption both at rest and in transit. We follow a zero-knowledge architecture — your documents are accessible only to you. We are compliant with India's data protection framework.
